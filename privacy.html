Top Wish – Privacy Policy

R Eaves Studios · Effective 30 October 2026

Top Wish collects no data at all. We don't see, store or share anything you or your children put into the app.

What stays on your device

Everything added to Top Wish (children's names, wishes, drawings, photos and letters) is stored only on your iPhone or iPad. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the app. You can also erase everything at any time with Start again in the Grown-ups area.

What the app doesn't have

No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code and no internet connection. The app works fully offline.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: used only to add a picture to a wish, and to save catalogue images to your Photos when a grown-up chooses to.
  • Notifications: local reminders on the device for Santa's letters. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Sharing

If a grown-up shares the gift list or catalogue, it goes through Apple's standard share sheet to the app they choose (for example Messages, WhatsApp or email). Top Wish doesn't send it anywhere itself.

Children

Top Wish is made for children aged 6 to 9 and their families. Because we collect nothing, no personal information about children is ever gathered.
