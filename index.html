Common questions

How do I get into the Grown-ups area?
Tap Grown-ups and enter the secret word. It keeps children out of the gift list.

When does Santa's letter arrive?
The morning after the list is posted, and a second letter arrives on Christmas Day.

Can more than one child use it?
Yes, up to four children can share one device, each with their own page.

Is my child's data safe?
Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no data collected, no ads. See our privacy policy.

How do I start again for next year?
Use Start again in the Grown-ups area. This erases everything on the device.
